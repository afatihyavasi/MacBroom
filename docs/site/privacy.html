Privacy notice

MacBroom does not collect, store or send any data about you. This page covers the app and this website.

The app

  • It runs entirely on your Mac. It makes no network connection and has no telemetry.
  • Deletion history is kept locally and can be viewed inside the app.
  • Nothing is deleted without a preview and your confirmation, apart from scheduled cleaning you switch on yourself.

This website

  • It has no analytics, no cookies and no forms.
  • It loads the Geist typeface from Google Fonts and the Phosphor icon set from jsDelivr. Those providers may see your IP address when your browser requests the files.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.
